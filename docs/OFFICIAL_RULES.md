# Official Rules Baseline

Primary source: California State Lottery Fantasy 5 official game page and approved regulations.

- Objective: select five winning numbers from a field of 39.
- Matching is irrespective of order.
- Draws take place every day after draw entry closes at 6:30 p.m.
- Official website publishes past winning numbers including draw date, draw number and winning numbers.
- Match 5 of 5 odds: 1 in 575,757.

Sources:
https://www.calottery.com/draw-games/fantasy-5
https://static.www.calottery.com/-/media/project/calottery/pws/pdfs/approved-regulations-12325-eff-040525415.pdf
